## First-release estimate and broad-parity uncertainty

The F18-F30 proposal's `12-20` estimate is a feature-behavior estimate after scope review, not a total backlog or evidence that F30 completes the project. First release remains approximately 31-45 accepted F-sized deliverable slices overall: 13 foundation/localization/route units (F0-F8 plus F14-F17, with F17 still local-only at this base) plus an estimated 18-32 future evidence, feature, security, integration and release slices for the six boundaries in `docs/fresh-start-reconciliation.md` (account/session, profile, one content read, one audited write, staff TOTP, personal guestbook) and presentation. Historical F9-F13 retries/evidence drafts are not counted again as new distinct accepted boundaries. Independent review cards are acceptance work for a slice, not extra feature units. F40 is later optional FAQ parity and therefore does not increase this first-release estimate or replace the accepted F5 content contract. This is a planning range, not a contiguous numbering promise or a completed release; grouping can change after owner decisions.

Broad parity is a separate and unresolved estimate: approximately 60-90+ F-sized units may be required if full public/community/forum breadth, housekeeping/CMS breadth, publication and media workflows, multiple themes/assets, operational release work, and integration evidence are included. The upper bound is not reliable. Pixel63, Atom, client handoff, emulator writes, payments, production operations, and SWF/Flash replacement remain optional or explicitly closed; they are not approved by this synthesis and are excluded from the first-release range.
