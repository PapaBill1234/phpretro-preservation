F37 staff TOTP step-up

fidelity: guessed

What works:
- Synthetic per-staff records are looked up by staff identity.
- RFC 6238 validation delegates computation to github.com/pquerna/otp using
  synthetic 6-digit, 30-second, HMAC-SHA1 parameters and accepts the current step.
- Adjacent steps are examined only to classify and reject them as expired; disabled, missing,
  malformed, wrong, and wrong-user inputs fail closed.
- Store failures return ErrStore before any state is written.

What is guessed:
- 30-second steps and zero accepted skew are synthetic policy assumptions, not
  established by retained evidence or owner approval.
- No replay state, rate limiting, audit persistence, or HTTP/session binding is
  implemented; these require the owner/security gate and follow-up design.
- SyntheticCode exists only to make deterministic fixture tests.
